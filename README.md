### 🚀 About Me

Biophysics MSc student exploring RNA structure, molecular mechanisms, and the intersection of computational biology, machine learning, and physics.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **RNA secondary structure prediction — investigating how physical and evolutionary constraints can improve ML-based models.**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **Machine learning, Python, computational RNA biology, molecular simulation, and Japanese.**  
🤔 &nbsp;I'm looking for help with **Making my computational models smarter without making them completely hate me.**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **RNA, biophysics, molecular dynamics, machine learning, computational biology, or why RNA is somehow both beautiful and incredibly annoying.**
